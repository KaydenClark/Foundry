## Outcome

The Schematic accurately explains the thirteen-Hall Foundry through one local,
versioned, public-safe Projection fixture. Its campus, reference views,
Governance explanation, and deterministic Workflow presentation agree on
permanent identities, movable homes, declared Sockets, and authority boundaries
without reading a repository, binding a Module, or changing Actuality.

## Why It Matters

The pre-S-017 Schematic presented a retired four-Hall arrangement. That
misstated the current Foundry architecture, hid the separate responsibilities
of Intake, Validation, Orchestration, Design, Knowledge, Scheduling,
Production, and Gauge, and weakened the visible distinction between Gatehouse
infrastructure, read-only judgment, observation, implementation, and
integration.

## Decisions And Contracts

- One immutable, versioned **local Projection fixture** is the only Schematic
  source for public architectural records. It projects, but does not import,
  the Foundry Canon.
- Every rendered Hall, Socket, and Module record carries an opaque permanent
  ID. A public architectural home relation may move without changing that ID;
  no private filesystem path is rendered or stored in the fixture.
- The native Hall roster is Intake, Validation, Gatehouse, Orchestration,
  Design, Knowledge, Scheduling, Forge, Production, Assay, Ward, Gauge, and
  provisional Shipping. Shipping remains provisional in name and only models
  packaging for a declared producer.
- Gatehouse supplies mandatory protected-passage infrastructure and one
  append-only receipt per passage. It never selects routes, validates, grants
  authorization, or writes Canon/Grounding.
- Validation emits read-only findings to Orchestration. Gauge observes receipt
  activity for CIC/responsible-Hall/Discord notification concepts and never
  writes Canon or Grounding.
- A deterministic workflow distinguishes Foundry-producer work through Forge
  from product/project work through Production; Design, Knowledge, and
  Scheduling are optional passages. Assay is read-only; Ward may make at most
  one bounded repair before integration, while `integration -> main` remains
  owner-only.
- The seven-view shell and six Governance Planes remain unchanged. S-017
  implements Workflow as one category-only Halls/Modules/Sockets plan with no
  FactoryStack. Governance alone retains the ordered FactoryStack plus one
  selected explanatory plan.
- The visual drift gate is a deterministic checked-in Projection signature and
  renderer-consumption contract, not a live lookup, browser snapshot hash, or
  a substitute for desktop visual acceptance.
