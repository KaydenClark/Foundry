Plate 6 — Plain English

What am I looking at?

FND-01 drew the Foundry as two factories with doors and helpers on wires. That was useful: it made the idea tangible. FND-02 keeps the factory metaphor but draws the ownership lines we have since built.

The factory itself is now a portable kit. Its four permanent rooms are the Forge, Assay, Ward, and Gatehouse. The kit also carries the crew contracts, scheduling mechanics, project and memory scaffolding, and deterministic tools needed to start cleanly somewhere else.

Optional helpers are still separate. They plug in through socket contracts; they are not hidden inside the walls. A deployed instance owns its actual projects, memory, credentials, bindings, schedules, and runtime evidence, so none of that private material leaks into the reusable product.

Most importantly, the public kit is no longer copied by hand. The Forge builds one exact artifact, the Assay independently checks that exact artifact, staging receives only the accepted tree, and a cold adoption proves it can wake up somewhere fresh.
