# Gatehouse Lexicon

- **Gatehouse:** the deterministic Hall that owns containment, Job Order
  validation, clearance enforcement, and shipping gates.
- **Job Order:** the named authorization packet for one Actuality change.
- **Grounding:** durable proof checked in against the same Job Order.
- **Shipping gate:** a deterministic or owner-held condition that must pass
  before work crosses its declared boundary.

Shared vocabulary remains canonical in GPT_OS root `LEXICON.md`.
