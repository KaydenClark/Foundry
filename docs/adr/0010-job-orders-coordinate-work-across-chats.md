# Job Orders coordinate work across chats

A Job Order is the durable coordination and tracking form of an Intent that cannot be completed in one chat. ([ADR-0021](0021-job-orders-are-a-form-not-a-precondition.md) narrows this: it is the form such work takes when an order is in force, not a precondition for the work existing.) Direct owner instruction assigns roles for work that fits in one chat; the order preserves roles and authority across sessions and agents, while authorization comes from Canon, Clearance, and owner scope.
