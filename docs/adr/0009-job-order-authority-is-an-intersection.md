# Job Order authority is bounded by its sources

A Job Order's effective authority is the intersection of current Canon, the issuer's Clearance, the assignee's and tools' permissions, and explicit owner scope. The order cannot create or waive Canon, import Intent as a requirement, or turn an unavailable future mechanism into a prerequisite, preserving the boundaries of the authority it carries.
