# Governance verbs name operations

Promote, ground, settle, project, and related verbs name operations distinguished by their targets and required evidence. Promote moves an artifact to its place in the Workbench Contract and does not inherently increase authority, so promotion still applies when the destination is Grounding or Enduring Context.

Consequences: New definitions for ground, settle, and the other verbs remain unsettled; the shared promote skill needs a later alignment pass.
