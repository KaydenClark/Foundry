# Active Agent Checklist

> **Status:** Provisional grilling reference — not Canon or an execution
> authorization. Promote deliberately if this becomes a binding Foundry
> contract.

Use this checklist whenever Captain, Steward, or Assistant needs to inspect an
active agent, chat, or team. It is the minimum information needed to supervise
work without rediscovering its context.

## Required at-a-glance context

- [ ] **Factory** — the Factory responsible for this work.
- [ ] **Job Order** — the owning Job Order and its current state.
- [ ] **Chat / team** — the active chat or team carrying the work.
- [ ] **Directing stance** — whether Steward or Assistant directs the team;
      Captain coordinates the overall effort.
- [ ] **Current task and state** — what is being done now and whether it is
      active, waiting, blocked, or complete.
- [ ] **Next action** — the concrete action needed for forward progress.
- [ ] **Authority and hard stops** — the work's allowed scope, owner gates,
      and any effect boundary that cannot be crossed autonomously.
- [ ] **Budget** — the applicable budget or limit, with current usage when it
      is available.
- [ ] **Latest result or blocker** — the newest meaningful outcome, evidence,
      or precise reason progress cannot continue.
- [ ] **Recovery link** — the current handoff or resume reference in the Job
      Order folder.

## Captain maintenance check

During Captain's daily scheduled maintenance, review each non-terminal Job
Order's active chats and handoffs. Ensure the checklist information is current
enough for another chat to resume safely; reconcile stale, duplicated, or
superseded handoffs without discarding needed recovery evidence.

## Operating rule

Captain coordinates the effort at the chat/team level. Steward and Assistant
direct their respective teams and inner agents. An active chat must remain
linked to one Job Order and one Factory.
